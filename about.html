Who we are

Café · Catering · Carry Out.

Oasis began in 1988 as a place where flavour mattered — and grew, occasion by occasion, into a full catering and event-management house. Today the same kitchen serves home-style café plates, grand wedding buffets and everything in between.

Our positioning has never changed: quality & taste, an experienced team, professional service, thoughtful planning, beautiful setups and memorable experiences — with attention to detail in every deg, every drape, every greeting.

Our journey

Three chapters

1988 — The beginning

Oasis opens its doors in Islamabad with a simple creed: good food, honestly served.

Over the years — The table grows

The café and carry-out kitchen earn their regulars; catering spreads by word of mouth from family dawats to large celebrations.

Today — The complete event house

Catering, tent service, décor, planning and entertainment — weddings, corporate evenings and private parties across the city.
