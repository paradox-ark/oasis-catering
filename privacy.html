Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. Oasis Catering and Event Management respects your privacy. This website requires no login, signups or account creation.

1. Information You Share

When you submit a quotation request or message us, you may provide your name, phone number, email, event date, guest count, and menu preferences. These details are used exclusively by our planning team to prepare itemized pricing and coordinate your booking.

2. What We Do Not Collect

We do not store customer profiles or tracking cookies. Your menu shortlist is stored only within your own browser (local storage) and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser data.

3. Third-Party Links

Our website provides direct links to WhatsApp, Google Maps, Instagram, and Email. When navigating away to these services, their respective privacy policies apply.
